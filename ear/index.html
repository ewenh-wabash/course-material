Even within music, the same word can mean multiple things (mostly electronic vs acoustic, but sometimes not)
  • Key (piano key, keyboard key, key of Bb)
  • Meter (measure loudness in recorded music, and also the grid of beats and measures)
  • Piano (quiet, and keyboard)
  • Canon (a collection of music of a certain kind, Pachelbel)
  • Modulate (change key, alter an electronic waveform)
  • Track (either a full piece of music - like on an album, or a single line of music within a full piece of music - like the flute line)
  • Tune (intonation vs a song)
  • Program (which pieces to play in a performance, the story of a piece of music, the software you use to make music, the specific settings of a synth)
  • Record (Heteronym - something you play vs how you capture audio)
  • Release (ADSR vs to launch a new album or project)
  • Sample (a small chunk of music for looping, a single number representing a unique speaker placement - ie samples per second)
  • Chorus (audio effect, type of performance ensemble)
  • Cutoff (to stop playing music, or the frequency where a filter kicks in)
  • Dynamic (loud/soft vs type of microphone)
