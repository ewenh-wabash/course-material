Low Pitch Sounds?
As you age, your ear's low frequency hearing does not naturally decay, like it does with high frequency hearing. However, there are lots of potential causes of low-frequency hearing loss.

Human ears can typically hear down to 20Hz, though producing a sound that low typically requires a high-end subwoofer. So the test below is probably more a test of your sound system than your ears, but it still may prove useful!
Frequency: 1000 Hz
Test Your 'Ear Age'
As your ear gets older, the upper range of your hearing naturally becomes more limited. This is a process that starts in childhood and progresses through your whole life!

Though not scientific, you can identify your 'ear age' by finding the highest pitch you can hear below. Move the slider up until you can't hear the sound anymore (then click it a few times to confirm).
