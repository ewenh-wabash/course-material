Quick Reference: Common Music Symbols

Duration

how long each note lasts

Symbol Name Length (in a 4/4 measure)
w whole note lasts 1 whole measure (4 beats)
h half note lasts 1/2 of a measure (2 beats)
q quarter note lasts 1/4 of a measure (1 beat)
e eighth note lasts 1/8 of a measure (1/2 beat)
x sixteenth note lasts 1/16 of a measure (1/4 beat)

Dotted Durations

each dot lengthens a note 1.5 x

Symbol Name Length (in a 4/4 measure)
w .
dotted whole note lasts 1.5 whole notes (6 beats), same length as 3 half notes
h .
dotted half note lasts 1.5 half notes (3 beats), same length as 3 quarter notes
q .
dotted quarter note lasts 1.5 quarter notes (1.5 beats), same length as 3 eighth notes
e .
dotted eighth note lasts 1.5 eighth notes (3/4 of a beat), same length as 3 sixteenth notes

Clef

how to tell which note belongs on which line/space

Symbol Name Meaning
= &
treble clef common for women's voices and many smaller instruments, the spiral curves around the "G" line
= ?
bass clef common for men's voices and many larger instruments, the dots surround the "F" line
= ã
percussion clef used for unpitched percussion, where no pitches are needed
= B
alto clef uncommon but often used with viola, the center points to the "C" line

Accidental

how to raise or lower the pitch of a note

Symbol Name Meaning
n natural do not raise/lower the pitch
# sharp raises the pitch by a half-step
b flat lowers the pitch by a half-step
‹ double sharp raises the pitch by 2 half-steps (aka a whole step)
∫ double flat lowers the pitch by 2 half-steps (aka a whole step)

Dynamics

how loud to play a section

Symbol Name Meaning
ñ niente silent
∏ triple piano (aka: pianississimo) very very quiet
π double piano (aka: pianissimo) very quiet
p piano quiet
P mezzo piano moderately quiet
F mezzo forte moderately loud
f forte loud
ƒ double forte (aka: fortissimo) very loud
Ïdv triple forte (aka: fortississimo) very very loud
fp forte-piano begin forte, then immediately play piano
ß sforzando play this one note much louder (same as Z)

Meter

how beats are grouped and counted in a measure

Symbol Name Meaning
4 4
common time c
(aka: simple quadruple time) 		4 quarter-note beats per measure
3 4
simple triple time 3 quarter-note beats per measure
2 4
simple duple time 2 quarter-note beats per measure
12 8
compound quadruple time 12 eighth notes per measure (4 groups of 3)
9 8
compound triple time 9 eighth notes per measure (3 groups of 3)
6 8
compound duple time 6 eighth notes per measure (2 groups of 3)
7 8
7-8 time an example of "complex meter" (any meter where the beats can't be divided evenly)

Articulations

how to play a note

Symbol Name Meaning
q >
accent play this note with emphasis
q .
staccato play this note shorter, detached from the next note
q -
tenuto play this note longer, almost no detachment from the last note
q v
marcato play this note with even more emphasis
q u
fermata stop counting time for a moment (hold the note, or extend the rest)
q Ÿ
trill quickly alternate between this note and the scale note above it
