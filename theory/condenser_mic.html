How a Condenser Microphone Works

Sound waves move a diaphragm, one plate of a polarized capacitor (the other plate is fixed) → the changing distance between the plates alters the capacitance, producing small fluctuations in voltage →
a powered preamp amplifies the voltage and lowers the impedance, allowing the signal to travel over long cables with minimal loss or noise.
48V phantom power is sent through the audio cable to power the preamp and often to polarize the capacitor plates.
