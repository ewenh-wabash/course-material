How the Cochlea Works

small hairs (cilia) inside the cochlea vibrate sympathetically with the pressure of a soundwave, sending an electrical signal to the brain.
The longest hairs are in the middle of the coil and are more responsive to low sounds.
The shortest hairs are towards the outside of the coil and are more responsive to high sounds.
The shorter hairs are the usually first to stop working due to age or overuse.
